PRIVACY POLICY
IntelliAttend SmartBoard

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IntelliAttend ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the IntelliAttend SmartBoard application ("Software").

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
   The Software collects the following types of information:

   a. Attendance Data: Student identification, check-in/check-out timestamps, classroom session details, and section/room assignments.

   b. Device Information: Device identifier, operating system version, network configuration, hardware specifications (for compatibility and crash recovery), and application version.

   c. Authentication Data: SmartBoard ID and credentials used to authenticate with our servers. Credentials are stored securely on-device using Windows DPAPI encryption.

   d. Usage Logs: Application startup logs, error reports, and performance metrics to help us improve the Software.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
   We use the collected information for the following purposes:
   a. Providing and maintaining the attendance tracking service.
   b. Processing and recording attendance data.
   c. Sending real-time updates and notifications to authorized users.
   d. Detecting and resolving technical issues.
   e. Improving the Software and user experience.
   f. Ensuring security and preventing unauthorized access.

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
   a. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
   b. Sensitive data on the device is encrypted using Windows DPAPI (Data Protection API).
   c. Server-side data is stored in encrypted databases with restricted access controls.
   d. We implement SSL certificate pinning to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks.

4. DATA SHARING
   We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only:
   a. With your educational institution's authorized administrators.
   b. As required by law or legal process.
   c. To protect the rights, property, or safety of IntelliAttend, our users, or the public.

5. DATA RETENTION
   a. Attendance records are retained as configured by your institution's administrators.
   b. Device and usage logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days.
   c. You may request data deletion by contacting your institution administrator or support@intelliattend.app.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
   You have the right to:
   a. Access the data collected about your institution's usage.
   b. Request correction of inaccurate data.
   c. Request deletion of your data, subject to institutional policies.
   d. Opt out of non-essential data collection (crash reports, usage analytics).

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
   The Software is designed for use in educational institutions. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without appropriate institutional consent and authorization.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
   We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through application notifications or to your institution's administrators.

9. CONTACT US
   For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your data rights, contact:
   Email: support@intelliattend.app

10. COMPLIANCE
    We comply with applicable data protection regulations including GDPR (where applicable) and FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) for educational institutions in the United States.
